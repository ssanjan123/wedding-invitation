Send the invitations

One personal link per guest, with their name on the envelope, and a WhatsApp message ready to send. Your list stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Which invitation

Guests

One guest per line: the name as it should appear on the envelope, then a comma and their WhatsApp number (optional). Bangladeshi numbers can be written as 017… or +880 17…; others need their country code. Rows pasted from a spreadsheet work too.

Message

{name} becomes the guest's name and {link} their personal link.
